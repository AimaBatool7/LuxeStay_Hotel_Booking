About Us

Quiet Luxury, Honestly Booked

LuxeStay began in 2018 with a simple frustration: booking a genuinely special hotel meant wading through thousands of near-identical listings. We built LuxeStay to be the opposite — a small, opinionated collection of properties worth traveling for, booked in minutes with no games around pricing.

Today we work with independent boutique hotels and international resorts across 24 countries, each one visited and reviewed by our team before it's listed.
